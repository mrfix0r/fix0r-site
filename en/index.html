ABOUT ME / FIX0R

Hey! I'm Alexander 😎

A programmer by training. Fix0r in games and on stream.

The dream that brought me to Twitch

I once dreamed of becoming a professional PUBG player. I put together my own team, played tournaments and occasionally streamed on Twitch — both competitive matches and casual games.

In 2018, I made it to tier 2, but didn't qualify for PGI in Berlin. Things didn't go to plan after that: I'd left my job for esports and was living on my savings. In 2019, I lost my footing for a year.

Then I came back with fresh energy, plans and ideas. Competitive PUBG was behind me. Next came Apex with some great players, movie nights and jokes whenever we could fit them in. The games change; the joy of hanging out stays.

More about my PUBG journey

I started as captain and shot-caller for RearGuarD, a team I built myself. I later played for Union, Per Aspera Ad Astra and Hello.

I had offers from WinStrike and CrowCrowd: the first didn't work out, and I turned down the second. Some former teammates went on to work at NaVi — not as players, just to be clear.

Back then, the stream was almost entirely about competitive PUBG. Other games only appeared during breaks when I needed a change of pace.

Glad you're here. Thanks for the support and motivation — I appreciate every one of you. If you feel at home here, stick around and follow!

02 / OUR CODE

Around the same table.

Three things that hold FC together.

  • I

    Play fair

    We recommend avoiding RMT: buying or selling in-game currency, items or accounts for real money may break a game's rules and lead to a permanent account ban.

    We prefer earning our progress and helping each other along the way.

    Cheats and the promotion of cheats are strictly forbidden in the community.

  • II

    Respect teammates and opponents

    Disagreements and jokes are welcome. Insults, bullying and toxic behavior stay outside.

  • III

    Stick together

    Help newcomers, keep your word and remember: there's a person on the other side of the screen.

✦ Honor in the game. Friendship beyond it.
